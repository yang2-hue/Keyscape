## enter

```text
Create one exceptional art-direction hero image for Keyscape, an interactive miniature world on a SINGLE Enter key. Use case: stylized-concept, photoreal miniature craftsmanship. Theme: ENTER — THE RAINY NIGHT BOOKSHOP. This is a world using a key as its canvas; the story and architecture must occupy 85% of the visual attention.

A precisely composed, physically plausible tiny independent bookshop, inside a modest weathered limestone and dark timber corner building, in deep blue evening rain. A wide open warmly glowing front door is the composition's focal point: a narrow wet cobbled path runs continuously from the front edge of the key directly across a little threshold into the shop. One side of the building is an intentional architectural cutaway showing rich walnut shelves packed with individual books, a tiny reading table and chair, a brass reading lamp, a winding stair to an intimate mezzanine. The cutaway is intentional like a beautifully crafted museum architectural model, not a destroyed wall. Under a small fabric awning: a few book crates and one umbrella, authentic restrained detail. The sculptural roof is lifted slightly at the back by a tall chimney, enough variation in silhouette. No clutter and no excessive props.

Canvas: one horizontal ANSI Enter key, roughly 2.25:1 plan ratio. A very thin warm ivory keycap plinth with softly tapered sides; its outline is subtly visible under the little world. The street and building use the full footprint, not a small model perched on a big empty key. A tiny unobtrusive engraved Enter return-arrow on the FRONT VERTICAL LIP ONLY connects this to a key. No keyboard, no other keys, no switches, no hands, no giant letters, no resin cube and no glass display case.

Visual language: sophisticated hand-built architectural maquette photographed by a world-class miniaturist; sharp joinery and convincingly tiny paper edges, stone mortar, burnished wood, rain beads, restrained moss. Warm amber light in the doorway reflected across blue slate wet paving, muted mineral teal night shadows, parchment highlights. Natural imperfect surfaces with believable roughness, avoid shiny plastic and generic toy render. One clear focal point, calm hierarchy, purposeful silhouette, a beautiful image even at thumbnail size. Soft rainy atmosphere localized around the model, sparse delicate rain streaks, no distracting particles.

Camera: close 3/4 view from slightly above the street, look INTO the glowing doorway and see the cutaway interior. Whole key/world visible with tasteful breathing room on a near-charcoal seamless studio background, gentle contact shadow, subtle cinematic rim light. Landscape 3:2 image. Maximum craft and architectural detail with crisp focal area and subtle depth of field, never blur away the entire world. No typography, labels, UI, collage, watermarks, or concept-sheet borders. Produce a single finished new concept image, not a screenshot of the existing coastal product.
```

## space

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Produce a new art-directed photoreal miniature hero image for Keyscape: SPACE — THE FAR-SIDE GREENHOUSE. One single elongated spacebar is a canvas for a tiny inhabitable lunar world. This should feel wondrous, quiet and exquisitely constructed, not a gaming keyboard product.

Scene: a long low strip of beautifully sculpted gray-white lunar regolith with one off-center impact crater. Across the length, an ivory lunar research habitat gradually transforms into a delicate ribbed greenhouse. The greenhouse is the focus: thin structural metal arches, clear individually fitted glass panels with restrained reflections, lush tiny edible plants and hanging leaves, warm practical lights, narrow interior walkway and a small bench. One tiny astronaut visible at the greenhouse airlock provides scale. A continuous lit walkway connects the habitat, airlock and greenhouse; realistic doors and access routes. A little blue distant Earth is visible in the black space just behind the miniature, tiny and subdued, not a huge fantasy planet. Small carved rock terraces and a short carefully placed solar array suggest life support. Use visual density mainly around the greenhouse; leave a calm sculpted lunar section to balance it. No clutter or random machinery.

The entire scene fits ONE long spacebar footprint, approximately 6.25:1, photographed diagonally so its beautiful length reads. The key base is just a 2mm thin pale porcelain edge beneath the lunar ground, tapering subtly like an actual long keycap, with a tiny engraved 'SPACE' only on the front vertical rim. No full keyboard, no other keys, no exposed switches. No resin water block, no thick display pedestal. Landscape 3:2 composition with whole object in frame, diagonal silhouette, three-quarter elevation showing greenhouse interior and moon surface. Calm near-black matte studio backdrop, soft surface contact shadow, sharply directed silvery lunar sidelight and exquisite amber greenhouse interior contrasting against deep lunar shadows. Muted chlorophyll greens, chalky lunar grays, warm amber. Natural scale, fine precision joinery, dusty regolith, thin glass edges, scientific rather than neon cyberpunk. Carefully controlled microdetail, magnificent focal hierarchy, tactile museum model craftsmanship. Not an illustration of the existing coastal island. No UI, labels, captions, collage, watermarks or giant lettering. The world is the subject, not an expensive keyboard.
```

## shift

```text
Create a single finished art-direction image, use case stylized-concept, photoreal miniature architecture and landscape, for the Keyscape project. Theme: SHIFT — THE SNOWLINE TRANSFER. A single Shift key is the canvas for a remarkable miniature alpine funicular. This image must stand beautifully as an artwork, with strong composition, visual narrative and exceptional tiny physical detail.

One asymmetric sculpted mountain ridge runs diagonally across a long single key footprint, approximately 2.75:1 in plan. At the low front end sits a warm small timber-and-stone mountain railway station, with a very small open platform and two human silhouettes for scale. A continuous funicular track ascends the slope in a beautiful clear diagonal, carrying one small deep vermilion red carriage midway to a higher station and a small silver astronomical observatory near the snowy summit. The railway must physically join both stations, with believable two rails, rail supports, a safe platform and an uninterrupted route; not a cable floating through scenery. A delicate stone stair and switchback footpath provides a second credible route cut into the rock. The ridge and stations are one connected sculptural composition with steep rocky sides; the composition should communicate upward movement and changing elevation, a spatial interpretation of Shift.

Lower rocks reveal weathered stratified gray limestone, sparse dark alpine pines and windblown snow. As the route climbs, snow gets deeper and the high station sits just above a very restrained thin ribbon of mist around the midpoint of the ridge. A tiny lit station window and signal lamp provide quiet warm detail. The red carriage is the single strong color accent; no colored gaming lights. Avoid a Christmas-village aesthetic, excessive chalets or random ornaments. All scenery stays within one key's footprint; use the whole surface, never a small generic mountain stuck onto a big empty keyboard key.

Key carrier: a thin ivory tapered keycap lip, subtly visible beneath the terrain, one very small engraved upward-arrow on its front vertical edge. No full keyboard, other keys, switches, hands, glass case, resin cube, logo or UI. The world's silhouette and path are the focus, not the hardware.

Camera and art direction: a low 3/4 elevated view from near the lower station, able to see the entire rail ascending to the summit. Whole object in frame, carefully balanced visual masses and negative space. Soft bone-white studio backdrop and contact shadow, crisp pale winter morning light from one side, delicate cool slate shadows, creamy snow and warm muted timber. Tangible hand-built museum maquette quality with razor-fine railings, exact architectural joints, individually crafted tiny pines, snow overhangs and rough rock stratification. Restrained cinematic contrast, no oversaturated cyan, no toy plastic. Clear focal hierarchy and controlled detail, subtle optical depth of field while retaining both station silhouettes. Landscape 3:2 image. No labels, words, captions, watermarks, panels or collage. This is a new independent single-key visual concept, not an existing product screenshot.
```
